Share sheet

A website cannot register its own icon in Instagram’s share sheet. A Shortcut named MapMyApps can, and that is this personal version.

  1. Open Shortcuts and make a new shortcut.
  2. Turn on Show in Share Sheet. Accept URLs, Safari web pages, and text.
  3. Add Open URLs. URL is this page plus ?add= and the Shortcut Input.
  4. Share a Reel or pin. Tap MapMyApps. Confirm the place. It lands on your map.

Navigate opens Apple Maps from your current location. Places stay on this phone.
